Site-specific Projects

A site-specific work begins with a conversation with the collector and with the relationship between the work, the space and the light.

Each project is developed in coherence with the artistic research on aluminum and with the language of chasing, through a unique process conceived in relation to the context and to the character of the space that will host the work.

  1. 01

    Initial dialogue

    Sharing the idea, the space and the needs of the project.

  2. 02

    Project development

    Defining the dimensions, the formal characteristics and the compositional balance of the work.

  3. 03

    Creation

    The work is made by hand using the aluminum chasing technique.

  4. 04

    Delivery and installation

    Once the work is finished, it is delivered and placed in the intended space.

Working aluminum by hand takes time and attention: for this reason only a limited number of works are made each year, preserving the quality of the creative process.
