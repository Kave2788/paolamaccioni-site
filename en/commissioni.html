Custom Commissions

Personalized Art

I create custom works maintaining coherence with my artistic research on aluminum chasing. Each project is developed as a unique commission in dialogue with the collector, conceived in relation to space, light, and the formal language that characterizes my work.

  1. 01

    First contact

    Share your idea or the context where the artwork will be placed.

  2. 02

    Definition

    We define dimensions, subject, and formal characteristics of the work.

  3. 03

    Creation

    The artwork is hand-made using the aluminum chasing technique.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    After an initial deposit, the artwork is completed and delivered.

Artworks created in limited numbers throughout the year to guarantee the time necessary for research and hand craftsmanship.
