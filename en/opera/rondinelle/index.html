Linee di passaggio

Small swallows perch on thin metal lines, transformed into an essential landscape.

Some remain still, others prepare for flight: the composition captures a moment suspended between stillness and movement.

The embossing of aluminium restores the lightness of forms, transforming an everyday gesture into a rhythmic and silent visual structure.

From the series Organic Form and Figure
