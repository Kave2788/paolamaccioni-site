Armatura primordiale

In Primordial Armour the surface of the aluminium is worked as a system of internal tensions, generating a structure that is built up by pressure and resistance.

The relief does not add volume, but makes it emerge from within the slab, transforming the plane into a three-dimensional element. The light flows over the ribs and highlights their balance, making visible the relationship between the protection and fragility of the material.
