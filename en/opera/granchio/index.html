Carapace

Through the embossing of the aluminium, the figure of the crab emerges from the metal surface with an essential and dynamic structure.

The open claws and symmetry of the body evoke the defensive tension typical of this sea creature.

The work transforms a coastal organism into a symbolic form, suspended between biological fragility and the resistance of matter.

From the series Organic Form and Figure
