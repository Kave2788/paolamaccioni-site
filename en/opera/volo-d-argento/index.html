Volo d'Argento

Volo d'Argento (Silver Flight) is an embossed sculpture on aluminium depicting a winged horse, a mythical figure that in this work takes on an essential and contemporary trait, but at the same time is a reminder of the mythological figure, reinterpreted with a clean, modern, unadorned stroke.

The sharp contours and stylised lines give shape to a creature suspended between strength and lightness, between matter and spirit.

The completely hand-treated aluminium reflects light, transforming itself depending on the viewing angle.

With a minimalist and timeless language, this creation evokes the overcoming of limits and the silent power of imagination.

Metal, usually cold and rigid, here becomes a living surface, traversed by shapes that speak of momentum, freedom and the desire to go beyond limits.

From the series Structure and Tension
