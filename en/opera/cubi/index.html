Cubi

Cubes originates from a cantilevered aluminium work organised according to a repeated modular structure.

The surface is constructed through folds and reliefs that generate an alternation of solids and recesses, creating an effect of varying depth depending on the light. The inserts interrupt the metallic continuity and reinforce the perception of geometric rhythm.

The work develops as an ordered system, where repetition is not decorative but constructive, and light variation becomes an active part of the composition.
