Verso l'Orizzonte

A sailing ship advances through the waves with its sails unfurled, propelled by an invisible force.

The embossing of the aluminium shapes the surfaces of the sails and the bow, transforming the metal material into movement and tension.

The boat becomes a symbol of journey and quest, a passage between balance and challenge, between the stillness of the sea and the unknown of the horizon.
