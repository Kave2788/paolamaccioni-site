Paesaggio d'Oriente

An essential landscape inspired by oriental aesthetics takes shape through the embossing of aluminium.

Mount Fuji emerges under a golden sun, while clouds and birds traverse a suspended, silent space.

The circular composition encloses the image as a contemplative vision, balancing nature, symbol and matter.
