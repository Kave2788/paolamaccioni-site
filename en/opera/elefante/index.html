Memoria

The head of an elephant emerges from the metal surface through the embossed aluminium work. The folds of the skin and the monumentality of the form evoke the presence of an ancient and powerful creature.

The work reflects on the relationship between memory, strength and continuity of life, transforming the image of the animal into a symbol of stability and wisdom.

From the series Forma Organica e Figura
